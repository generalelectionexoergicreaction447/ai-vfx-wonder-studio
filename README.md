This application automates visual effects production. It replaces 3D characters within your existing video files. You do not need experience in animation or complex software to use the tool. The system handles lighting, motion, and character placement for you. 

## ⚙️ System Requirements

Your computer needs specific hardware to run this software. Ensure your machine meets these marks before you begin:

*   Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   Processor: Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 or better.
*   Memory: 16 GB of RAM.
*   Graphics Card: An NVIDIA graphics card with at least 8 GB of video memory.
*   Storage Space: 10 GB of free space on your hard drive.

If your computer uses an integrated graphics chip, the software might run slow or fail to process your files. A dedicated graphics card provides the best results for rendering your character replacements.

## 🚀 Preparing Your Video Files

The application performs best when your source video meets specific quality standards. Clean footage yields clear 3D character integration.

*   Resolution: Use 1080p or 4K video.
*   Frame Rate: Use 24, 30, or 60 frames per second.
*   Lighting: Ensure your subject is visible and not obscured by shadows.
*   File Format: Use MP4 or MOV files.

Close other heavy programs like video editors or games before you launch the software. This clears your computer memory for the rendering task.

## 🛠️ Using the Software

You can process your first video by following these steps inside the application:

1. Launch ai-vfx-wonder-studio from your Windows Start menu.
2. Click the Import button.
3. Select the video file from your computer.
4. Choose the 3D character you want to place in your scene from the character library.
5. Review the alignment preview to confirm the character matches the movement of your actor.
6. Click the Process button.
7. Monitor the progress bar until the task reaches 100%.
8. Save your output file to your preferred folder.

The application uses an automated process to track the actor. It maps the 3D character to the motion of the scene. You may notice small delays while the software calculates the background replacement. This is normal behavior during the rendering phase.

## 💡 Troubleshooting Common Issues

Check these items if you have trouble running the software:

*   App does not start: Right-click the icon and choose Run as Administrator.
*   Error during rendering: Ensure your graphics card drivers are current. Visit the website of your graphics card manufacturer to download the latest updates.
*   Low performance: Close background applications that consume memory. 
*   Missing character previews: Check your internet connection. The application occasionally pulls character assets from the cloud.

If you encounter a specific error number, copy that text and save it to a file. This helps you identify the exact cause of the stop. You can also restart your computer to clear temporary faults.

## 📂 Managing Your Project Files

The software gathers temporary data in a dedicated workspace folder. You can change this location in the Settings menu. Check this folder after you finish a project to delete large temporary files. This recovers disk space on your drive. 

## 🛡️ Privacy and Data

The software processes your video files locally on your computer. Your files stay on your machine. The system does not upload your video content to public servers for processing. This protects your creative work and keeps your footage private.
